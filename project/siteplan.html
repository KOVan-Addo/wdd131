Egg-Inn Website Plan

This planning document outlines the purpose, audience, visual design, typography, and proposed page layout for the Egg-Inn website.

1. Site Name

Egg-Inn

The name Egg-Inn is short, memorable, and friendly. It combines the word "Egg" with "Inn" to create a welcoming brand identity for an egg business. The name communicates the main product while giving the business a distinctive identity that customers can easily remember.

Tagline: Fresh Eggs. Better Choices.

2. Site Purpose

The purpose of the Egg-Inn website is to provide customers with information about the business and its fresh egg products. The website will showcase available egg packages, provide information about product sizes and pricing, explain the benefits of choosing Egg-Inn, and allow customers to submit an order or inquiry through an online form. The website will also provide contact information and other relevant business details to help customers make informed purchasing decisions.

3. Target Audience and Scenarios

The primary audience will include individuals, families, restaurants, food vendors, and small businesses looking for fresh eggs in convenient quantities.

Scenario 1

What egg packages and quantities are available from Egg-Inn?

Scenario 2

How can I place an order for eggs or request information about an order?

Scenario 3

How can I contact Egg-Inn if I want to purchase eggs for my restaurant or small business?

4. Color Scheme

The Egg-Inn color scheme is designed to communicate freshness, quality, warmth, and an agricultural connection while maintaining good readability and contrast.

Deep Green

#2F5D50

Used for the header, navigation, footer, major headings, and primary branding elements.

Golden Yellow

#E9B949

Used for buttons, highlights, accents, and calls to action.

Cream

#FFF9ED

Used primarily for the page background and light content areas.

Dark Charcoal

#2B2B2B

Used for body text and other content where strong readability is required.

5. Typography

Poppins

Poppins will be used for the site name, headings, navigation, buttons, and other prominent interface elements. Its clean, modern appearance will help establish the Egg-Inn brand.

Open Sans

Open Sans will be used for body paragraphs, product descriptions, form labels, scenarios, and other general content. It provides clear and comfortable reading across mobile and larger screens.

6. Proposed Website Pages

Home — index.html

The home page will introduce Egg-Inn with a hero section, business introduction, featured products, reasons to choose Egg-Inn, and calls to action leading visitors to the products and order pages.

Our Eggs — products.html

The products page will present available egg packages, quantities, prices, descriptions, and availability. JavaScript will dynamically display product information.

Order — order.html

The order page will provide an HTML form that allows customers to submit their name, contact information, preferred product, quantity, and additional requests.

References — references.html

The references page will identify external sources used for images, information, or other content included in the final website.
